# Batch cooking

Cooking a big pot takes barely longer than cooking a small one, and costs less energy per portion. Two sessions a week, about two hours on Sunday and one on Wednesday, are enough for a week of healthy lunches and dinners.

## A two-session plan

**Sunday (about 2 hours)**

1. Put the soaked beans on to boil first (they take longest) for the *ciorbă de fasole*.
2. While they cook, chop onions, carrots and peppers for both the soup and the cabbage. Chopping everything once saves time.
3. Start the *varză călită cu pui* in a second pot.
4. Make a *salată de varză*; it keeps 3–4 days.
5. Cook a pot of oats for the next two breakfasts, or prepare overnight oats.

**Wednesday (about 1 hour)**

1. Lentil stew (*mâncare de linte*): no soaking needed.
2. *Ghiveci* in the oven, or on the stove.
3. *Salată de sfeclă*, with the beetroot roasted in the oven together with the *ghiveci*.

Fresh, quick meals (omelette, mămăligă with cheese and eggs, mackerel with potatoes) fill the remaining days.

## Equipment

- **One large pot** (6–8 litres) for soups and stews.
- **A pressure cooker** (*oală sub presiune*) or an electric multicooker: beans in 25 minutes instead of 90.
- **Food containers**: glass or BPA-free plastic, in portion sizes, for the fridge and freezer.
- **A sharp knife and a large board**: chopping is most of the work.

## The energy cost

Cooking uses energy, and energy has a price. For an appliance of power $P$ (in kW) running for $t$ hours:

$$
\text{cost} = P \times t \times c
$$

where $c$ is your price per kWh. Find it on your electricity or gas bill: it is the *final* price, with all taxes and fees, divided by the kWh consumed. The values below are **examples**; use your own.

With electricity at an example $c = 1.30$ lei/kWh, simmering beans for 90 minutes on a 1.5 kW hob at half power (about 0.75 kW on average):

$$
0.75 \times 1.5 \times 1.30 \approx 1.46\ \text{lei}
$$

In a pressure cooker, 25 minutes at about 0.75 kW:

$$
0.75 \times \tfrac{25}{60} \times 1.30 \approx 0.41\ \text{lei}
$$

On a gas hob, the same calculation uses the gas price per kWh, which is usually much lower than electricity. Either way, energy is a real but small part of the cost: about 1 leu for a pot of 6 portions. Batch cooking halves it per portion, and **lids** reduce it further, since an uncovered pot needs considerably more energy to keep simmering.

## Freezing portions

Soups, bean and lentil stews, *ghiveci*, *varză călită*, *sarmale* and *tocăniță* all freeze well. Potatoes in a stew can become slightly grainy. Label each container with the dish and the date. A freezer with 6–8 portions of different dishes is the best defence against ordering food on a tired evening, which costs 35–60 lei a meal.
