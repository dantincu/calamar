# Soups: ciorbă de fasole

A *ciorbă* is a sour soup, soured with *borș*, lemon, sauerkraut juice or vinegar. It is the heart of the Romanian lunch, and one of the cheapest ways to eat vegetables.

## Ciorbă de fasole (bean soup)

**6 portions · ≈ 1.96 lei per portion · 258 kcal, 13 g protein, 10 g fiber**

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| dry white beans, soaked overnight | 300 g |
| onion | 150 g (1 large) |
| carrots | 150 g (2) |
| bell pepper | 100 g (1 small) |
| canned chopped tomatoes | 400 g (1 can) |
| sunflower oil | 30 g (3 tbsp) |
| *borș* | 500 ml |
| *leuștean* (lovage) and dill | 20 g |
| salt | 4 g (less than a teaspoon) |
| savory (*cimbru*), 2 bay leaves, pepper | |

1. Drain the beans, cover with fresh water, boil hard for 10 minutes, then drain again ("two waters").
2. Cover with 2.5 litres of fresh water, add the bay leaves and the savory, and simmer, covered, for about an hour, until almost soft.
3. Meanwhile dice the onion, carrots and pepper. Soften them in the oil, with a few spoons of water, for 10 minutes, without browning.
4. Add the vegetables and the tomatoes to the beans. Simmer 20 minutes more.
5. Bring the *borș* to the boil in a separate pan (this keeps the soup clear), skim it, and add it to the soup. Boil 2–3 minutes.
6. Season with salt and pepper, and add the chopped lovage and dill off the heat.

The beans, the most nutritious ingredient, are only a quarter of the cost. In August, when field tomatoes cost about 5 lei/kg, 400 g of fresh tomatoes (about 2 lei) can replace the canned ones, which takes the portion to about 1.6 lei.

## Variations

- **Ciorbă de legume** (vegetable soup): leave out the beans, and add potatoes, celeriac, green beans, peas or whatever needs using up. Add a handful of rice or vermicelli to make it more filling.
- **Ciorbă de perișoare** (meatball soup): small meatballs of minced chicken or pork with rice; to keep it cheap and lean, use 300 g of meat for 6 portions, and more vegetables.
- **Supă de pui** (chicken soup): after a meal of chicken legs, boil the bones with onion, carrot, parsnip and celery for 1–2 hours. Strain, then cook noodles or vegetables in the stock.
- **Spring nettle soup** (*ciorbă de urzici*): blanch 500 g of nettles, chop, and cook with onion, a little rice and *borș*; finish with a beaten egg or yogurt.

## Serving

Serve with wholemeal bread, or with a green chili and a spoon of yogurt instead of sour cream (*smântână*, which has three to six times as much fat). Soups keep 3–4 days in the fridge and freeze well; add the herbs fresh when reheating.
