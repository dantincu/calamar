# Stews: lentils, potatoes and ghiveci

Romanian *mâncăruri* (stews) are cooked slowly with onion, a little oil and tomato. They are naturally *de post* (vegan), keep for days, and reheat well.

## Mâncare de linte (lentil stew)

**4 portions · ≈ 2.31 lei per portion · 375 kcal, 21 g protein, 11 g fiber**

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| green or brown lentils | 300 g |
| onion | 150 g |
| carrots | 150 g |
| garlic | 3 cloves (15 g) |
| canned tomatoes | 400 g |
| sunflower oil | 25 g |
| parsley | 10 g |
| salt | 3 g |
| bay leaf, thyme, paprika | |

1. Rinse the lentils. Cover them with 1 litre of water, add the bay leaf, and simmer 20 minutes.
2. In another pan, soften the chopped onion and grated carrot in the oil, with a little water, for 10 minutes. Add the garlic and the paprika for the last minute.
3. Add the vegetables and the tomatoes to the lentils. Simmer 10–15 minutes, until the lentils are soft and the stew is thick.
4. Season, and add parsley.

It is the most protein-rich of the vegan main dishes (21 g for 2.31 lei); only *fasole bătută* gives more protein per leu. And the vitamin C in the tomatoes helps absorb their iron.

## Tocăniță de cartofi (potato stew)

**4 portions · ≈ 1.94 lei per portion · 309 kcal, 7 g protein, 8 g fiber**

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| potatoes | 1.2 kg |
| onions | 200 g |
| bell pepper | 150 g |
| tomato paste (*bulion*) | 70 g |
| garlic | 10 g |
| sunflower oil | 30 g |
| dill and parsley | 15 g |
| salt | 4 g |
| paprika, bay leaf, thyme | |

1. Soften the chopped onion and pepper in the oil for 10 minutes.
2. Add the paprika, the tomato paste, and the potatoes in large cubes. Cover with water.
3. Simmer, covered, for 25–30 minutes, until the potatoes are soft and some have broken down to thicken the sauce.
4. Add garlic, dill and parsley at the end.

It is low in protein, so serve it with an egg, a salad and some cottage cheese, or a portion of *fasole bătută*. With a pickle (*murătură*) on the side, it is a classic winter meal.

## Ghiveci (vegetable stew)

**5 portions · ≈ 3.18 lei per portion · 244 kcal, 7 g protein, 10 g fiber, 373 g of vegetables**

*Ghiveci* is the late-summer and autumn stew of whatever the market has in abundance. It is the best dish in the course for vegetables: almost the whole day's 400 g in one portion.

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| potatoes | 400 g |
| eggplant | 400 g |
| zucchini | 400 g |
| peppers | 300 g |
| onions | 200 g |
| carrots | 150 g |
| frozen peas | 200 g |
| canned or fresh tomatoes | 400 g |
| sunflower oil | 40 g |
| dill, parsley | 20 g |
| salt | 4 g |

1. Cut everything into large cubes. Salt the eggplant for 15 minutes, rinse and squeeze (optional, removes bitterness).
2. Soften the onion and carrot in the oil. Add potatoes and peppers, then 10 minutes later the eggplant and zucchini.
3. Add the tomatoes, a glass of water and the peas. Cover and simmer for 30 minutes, or bake in the oven at 180 °C for 45 minutes.
4. Finish with herbs.

Out of season, use frozen mixed vegetables for half the vegetables: the cost stays about the same, and so does the nutrition.
