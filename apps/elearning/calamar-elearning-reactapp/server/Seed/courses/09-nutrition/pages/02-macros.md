# Macronutrients

Protein, carbohydrates and fats provide all of your energy, and each also has a job beyond energy. This chapter covers how much of each you need, and why the *kind* matters as much as the amount.

The reference ranges, as a share of total energy (EFSA, WHO):

| Nutrient | Target |
|---|---|
| Protein | at least 0.83 g per kg of body weight (≈ 10–20 % of energy) |
| Carbohydrate | 45–60 % of energy; free sugars under 10 % |
| Fiber | at least 25 g/day |
| Fat | 20–35 % of energy; saturated fat under 10 %; trans fat as low as possible |

To convert a share of energy into grams, divide by the energy per gram. For 2000 kcal with 30 % from fat:

$$
\text{fat} = \frac{0.30 \times 2000}{9} \approx 67\ \text{g}
$$
