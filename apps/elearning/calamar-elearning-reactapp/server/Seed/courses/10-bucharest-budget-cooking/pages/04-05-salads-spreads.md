# Salads and spreads

## Salată de varză (cabbage and carrot salad)

**4 portions · ≈ 0.69 lei per portion · 91 kcal, 4 g fiber, 159 g of vegetables**

The cheapest dish in the course, and one of the most nutritious: raw cabbage is rich in vitamin C and fiber.

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| white cabbage | 600 g |
| carrots | 150 g |
| sunflower oil | 20 g |
| vinegar | 30 ml |
| dill | 10 g |
| salt | 2 g |

1. Shred the cabbage very finely. Sprinkle with the salt and squeeze it with your hands for a minute, until it softens and releases juice. This is the Romanian secret of a tender cabbage salad.
2. Add the grated carrot, the vinegar, the oil and the dill. Mix.

It keeps 3–4 days in the fridge, and gets better on the second day.

## Salată de sfeclă (beetroot salad with garlic)

**4 portions · ≈ 0.93 lei per portion · 122 kcal, 5 g fiber, 170 g of vegetables**

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| beetroot | 800 g |
| garlic | 2 cloves |
| sunflower oil | 20 g |
| vinegar | 30 ml |
| salt | 2 g |
| caraway seeds (optional) | |

1. Bake the beetroot whole, wrapped in foil, at 200 °C for about an hour (or boil it for 40–60 minutes), until a knife goes in easily. Bake it together with something else to share the oven's energy.
2. Peel when cool (wear gloves, or the color stays on your hands), and grate or slice.
3. Mix with crushed garlic, vinegar, oil, salt and caraway. Leave it for an hour.

Beetroot can turn urine and stools red. That is harmless.

## Fasole bătută (bean dip) with caramelized onion

**6 portions · ≈ 0.94 lei per portion · 315 kcal, 16 g protein, 11 g fiber**

The best value in the course: 17 g of protein for each leu. A traditional fasting dish, served with bread and pickles.

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| dry white beans, soaked | 400 g |
| onions | 200 g |
| garlic | 3 cloves |
| sunflower oil | 50 g |
| tomato paste | 30 g |
| salt | 4 g |
| bay leaf, paprika | |

1. Cook the beans with the bay leaf until very soft (see *Cooking dry legumes*). Drain, but keep a cup of the cooking water.
2. Blend or mash the beans with the garlic, salt and 2 tablespoons of the oil, adding cooking water until smooth and light.
3. Slice the onions thinly and cook them slowly in the rest of the oil for 15–20 minutes, until golden and sweet. Add the paprika and the tomato paste for the last minute.
4. Spread the beans on a plate, and pour the onion on top.

Traditional versions use twice as much oil. This one is lighter, and the onion still makes it.

## Zacuscă

The Romanian autumn spread: roasted eggplant and peppers (*kapia* or *gogoșari*), cooked slowly with onion, tomato paste and oil, then bottled for winter. In September, when eggplant costs about 3 lei/kg and peppers 5–8 lei/kg at Obor, a homemade jar costs a fraction of a shop-bought one, and you control the oil and salt. Store-bought zacuscă often has 10–20 g of fat per 100 g: check the label.

## Other cheap salads

- **Salată de vinete** (eggplant salad): roast the eggplant whole until black, peel, chop, and mix with onion, a little oil, and salt. A summer and autumn classic.
- **Salată de cartofi** with onion, pickles and a little oil and vinegar: a lighter alternative to mayonnaise salads.
- **Murături asortate**: sauerkraut, pickled peppers and green tomatoes, a winter salad without cooking. Rinse them to remove some salt.
- **Salată de fasole** (bean salad): cooked beans, red onion, parsley, oil and vinegar.
