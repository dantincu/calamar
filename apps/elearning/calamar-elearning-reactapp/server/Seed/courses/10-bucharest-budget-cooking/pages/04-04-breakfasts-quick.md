# Breakfasts and quick meals

## Terci de ovăz (oat porridge with apple and walnuts)

**1 portion · ≈ 3.58 lei · 556 kcal, 19 g protein, 10 g fiber**

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| oats | 60 g |
| milk | 250 ml |
| apple | 1 (150 g) |
| walnuts | 15 g (a small handful) |
| cinnamon | |

1. Bring the milk and the oats to the boil, stirring. Simmer 3–5 minutes, until creamy.
2. Grate in half the apple; dice the other half on top with the chopped walnuts and cinnamon.

**Overnight oats**: mix the oats with the milk (or with yogurt) in a jar the evening before, and leave it in the fridge. No cooking, ready to take to work.

The milk is half the cost. With 250 ml of water instead, the portion costs 1.83 lei, but has 8 g less protein and much less calcium; half milk, half water is a good compromise.

## Mămăligă cu brânză și ou (polenta with cheese and eggs)

**2 portions · ≈ 4.26 lei per portion · 683 kcal, 28 g protein**

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| cornmeal (*mălai*) | 200 g |
| water | 800 ml |
| telemea | 60 g |
| eggs | 4 |
| plain yogurt | 200 g |
| sunflower oil | 1 tsp |

1. Bring the water to the boil, with a pinch of salt only if your cheese is mild. Pour in the cornmeal slowly, whisking, so no lumps form.
2. Cook on low heat for 15–20 minutes, stirring often with a wooden spoon, until thick and it comes away from the sides.
3. Meanwhile, fry the eggs in a non-stick pan with the oil, or poach them.
4. Serve the mămăligă with the crumbled telemea, the eggs and the yogurt (instead of *smântână*).

This is a hearty meal for a cold day, or after physical work, but it has no vegetables. Add a salad, or some pickles in winter.

## Omletă cu spanac (spinach omelette)

**2 portions · ≈ 4.46 lei per portion · 335 kcal, 24 g protein, 186 g of vegetables**

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| eggs | 5 |
| frozen spinach | 300 g |
| onion | 80 g |
| sunflower oil | 10 g |
| telemea | 40 g |
| pepper, nutmeg | |

1. Soften the onion in the oil. Add the spinach and cook until the water has evaporated.
2. Beat the eggs with pepper and a pinch of nutmeg, pour them over the spinach, and crumble the telemea on top.
3. Cook on low heat, covered, until set, about 6–8 minutes. No salt needed: the cheese brings it.

A 10-minute dinner. Any vegetable works: peppers, zucchini, mushrooms, leftover potatoes.

## Iaurt cu fructe și semințe (yogurt with fruit and seeds)

**1 portion · ≈ 3.31 lei · 272 kcal, 11 g protein**

200 g of plain yogurt, 150 g of seasonal fruit (plums, apples, pears, berries), and 15 g of sunflower seeds. Flavored yogurts cost more and contain about 10–15 g of added sugar per pot; add fruit instead.

## More quick, cheap meals

- **Pâine cu brânză de vaci și roșii**: wholemeal bread with cottage cheese, tomatoes and spring onion.
- **Ouă cu cartofi**: leftover boiled potatoes, fried in a little oil with onion, and eggs.
- **Paste cu ton sau sardine**: pasta, a can of fish, canned tomatoes, garlic and parsley in 15 minutes.
- **Clătite** (pancakes): flour, milk and eggs, filled with cottage cheese and fruit, or spinach.
- **Hummus** with vegetables: cooked chickpeas, a spoon of tahini (or sunflower seeds blended), garlic, lemon.
