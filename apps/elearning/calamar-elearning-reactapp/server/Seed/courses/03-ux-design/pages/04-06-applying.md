# Applying the palette

Scales are raw material. This sub-chapter turns them into something components can use safely:

1. **Design tokens**: a layer of semantic names between the scales and the components.
2. **Dark mode**: a second mapping of the same tokens.
3. **States and transparency**: hover, pressed, focus, disabled and overlays, with the compositing formula.
4. **Color vision deficiencies**: designing for the 1 in 12 men and 1 in 200 women who see color differently.
5. **Testing and documenting** the palette.

The attached `build-palette.js` does steps 1 and 2 and checks the result, and `palette-demo.html` shows it in use.
