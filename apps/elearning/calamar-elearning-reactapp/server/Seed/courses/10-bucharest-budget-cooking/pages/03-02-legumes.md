# Cooking dry legumes

Beans, lentils and chickpeas are the foundation of a cheap, healthy diet: protein, fiber, iron, folate and slow-release carbohydrate, for about 9–12 lei per kilogram of dry weight.

## Dry or canned?

A 400 g can of beans holds about 240 g of cooked beans and costs about 4 lei. Dry beans roughly **double and a half** in weight when cooked. So per kilogram of **cooked** beans:

$$
\text{canned: } \frac{4}{0.24} \approx 16.7\ \text{lei/kg}, \qquad \text{dry: } \frac{9}{2.4} \approx 3.75\ \text{lei/kg} \ (+\ \text{energy, about } 0.2 \text{ lei})
$$

Dry beans cost about a quarter as much. Canned beans are still a good emergency food; rinse them to remove some of the salt.

## Cooking times

| Legume | Soak | Simmer | Pressure cooker | Weight gain |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| white beans (*fasole albă*) | 8–12 h | 60–90 min | 20–25 min | × 2.3–2.5 |
| chickpeas (*năut*) | 8–12 h | 90–120 min | 30–40 min | × 2.2 |
| green or brown lentils (*linte verde*) | no | 25–35 min | not needed | × 2.5 |
| red lentils (*linte roșie*) | no | 15–20 min (they fall apart: soups) | not needed | × 2.5 |
| split peas (*mazăre uscată*) | optional | 40–60 min | 15 min | × 2.3 |

Old beans take longer, sometimes twice as long. Buy from shops with a fast turnover.

## The method, for beans

1. **Sort and rinse**: look for small stones.
2. **Soak** in plenty of cold water, at least three times their volume, overnight. In a hurry: boil for 2 minutes, then leave them in the hot water, covered, for 1 hour.
3. **Drain and rinse.** Soaking water carries some of the sugars that cause gas.
4. **Boil hard for at least 10 minutes** (this destroys the toxic lectins), then simmer, covered, until soft: a bean should squash easily against the roof of your mouth.
5. **The Romanian "two waters" method** (*fierbere în două ape*): after the first boil, pour off the water and start again with fresh boiling water. It also reduces gas.
6. **Salt at the end**, or in the last 15 minutes. Acid (tomatoes, *borș*, vinegar) slows softening, so add it once the beans are soft.

Savory (*cimbru*), bay leaves and a little cumin are the traditional flavorings, and they also help digestion.

## About the gas

Legumes contain oligosaccharides, sugars that the gut bacteria ferment. It gets much better within a few weeks of eating legumes regularly, as the gut adapts. Start with small portions, soak and rinse well, and chew slowly.

## Batch and freeze

Cook a whole kilogram of beans at once. Divide them into 300–400 g portions (with some cooking liquid), and freeze. Each portion is a soup, a *fasole bătută*, a salad or a stew on a busy day.

## Quick check

You cook 500 g of dry white beans and get 1.2 kg. A portion of bean stew uses 200 g of cooked beans. How many portions, and how much do the beans cost per portion at 9 lei/kg?

$$
\frac{1200}{200} = 6 \text{ portions}, \qquad \frac{0.5 \times 9}{6} = 0.75\ \text{lei per portion}
$$
